Kuiper War · Overview

Small ships. Enormous odds.

A space combat game set in the Kuiper Belt. Small, fast ships thread asteroid fields to bring down vast fortress stations.

Beyond Neptune, the light is thin and the rock is everywhere. Players pilot agile ships through drifting asteroid fields, picking their moment to strike at heavily armed ring stations that glow red with power.

The visual language is built on contrast: cold blue engines for the player, hot red circuitry for the enemy. You always know who is who — even in the chaos of an asteroid field.

If Only · Overview

Lose a game, and you play it again.

The Heavenly Game — a story driven dice game. A mother plays hand after hand against a demon, while somewhere behind her a monitor keeps counting.

Each level moves the story somewhere new. Between hands, the demon talks — teasing, bargaining, waiting. The rules are familiar dice-scoring; the stakes are not.

ASA · Overview

Technology should meet people where they are.

ASA is an accessibility app designed so that one tap is enough to be heard — turning simple gestures into communication and care.

The icon tells the story: a fingertip, a ripple of sound, a heart. ASA is built for people for whom everyday technology is too fiddly, too fast, or too small — and for the families and carers around them.

One touch

Every core action is reachable with a single, generous tap — no precise gestures required.

Be heard

Clear feedback in sound and sight, so the person using it always knows they've been understood.

Built with care

Calm colours, high contrast and no clutter — designed to reduce stress, not add to it.

Healthtech

Design for every body.

Accessibility isn't a feature to add later. I build tools like ASA starting from the people who are usually left out — large targets, clear feedback, nothing assumed.
